At OSTA Trading GmbH we combine our decades of professional experience in pharmaceutical industry and established network of international manufacturers, to create every opportunity for you to succeed. We tailor our services to your needs to save your time and your costs.

Whether you require a product for an established commercial project or you are searching for a new product for your developing project, our highly experienced team will assist you with any request, guaranteeing to offer high-quality products at competitive prices. Support throughout
the entire
supply process

We deliver our best. Starting with your first inquiry through to commercial production, our team takes pride in supporting you at every stage of the supply process. From manufacturer and product sourcing, to providing logistics solutions, temperature-controlled storage and regulatory support. Thanks to our expertise and experience inside the pharmaceutical market, we built an established network of manufacturers across Europe and worldwide. This allows us to offer flexible solutions that are tailored to each of your project's individual technical, regulatory and commercial requirements.

The established manufacturer network gives us a basis for negotiating the competitive prices and finding alternatives when costs and availability change. We always consider all the practical terms of supply for our clients. From the prices and minimum order quantities to the lead times and frequency of your deliveries, we will do everything to meet your production needs.

Professional API Sourcing

Our team combines our scientific and market understanding with our hands-on experience to help you select, apply and search for specialty ingredients that are right for you. Committed to go beyond what is offered in our portfolio, we guarantee to find and source products you require.

Product Evaluation Before Purchase

Before you commit to order, we will arrange samples for laboratory testing or trial quantities for broader-scale testing any time they are required. All our samples will always be accompanied by the appropriate documentation.

Reliable Order Management

We offer temperature-controlled storage and transportation tailored to your products' requirements along with efficient logistics to reduce lead times and the full traceability of supply chain. Our aim is to optimise your costs and accelerate the time to market of your products.

Regulatory and Complaints Support

Here to help you identify all the documentation you need to ship to your specific market and manage customs procedures for your shipments. We also guarantee to assist in resolving any quality complaints, whether for investigation or recall.
